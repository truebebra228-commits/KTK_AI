Visual and movement update:
- An irregular crack in the ruined building marks each challenge exit.
  Entering the opening triggers the existing fade into the next room.
- The world draws at 960 x 540 in the 1280 x 720 logical play area, with
  nearest-neighbor scaling and separate full-resolution UI.
- Small pixel dust puffs form at the feet on jump and landing. Bomb blasts
  emit a larger plume. Landing emits dust instead of sparks.
- Continuous masonry below platforms conceals accidental gaps in the art.
  Small visual bevels and seeded moss, grasses and roots soften platform
  silhouettes. Platform collisions still follow the room layout.
- The backdrop, terrain, characters and effects share one camera shake.
- Both procedural arms are 4 logical pixels thick from shoulder to wrist.
  Their palms and finger marks retain separate shapes.
- Jump speed is 425 px/s, down from 450 px/s. Default bomb push is 1010,
  down from 1080. Room geometry is unchanged.

Menu and interface:
- Startup opens Play and Settings while gameplay is stopped.
- Actual mouse/keyboard menu input cannot move the player or place a bomb.
- Play starts room one without leaking its click into bomb placement.
- Settings expose sound volume and fullscreen. Volume changes the gain of
  actual audio voices; zero stops/silences them, raising it enables effects.
  Volume and fullscreen choices persist to user settings.
- Pause offers Resume, Settings and Main menu. Settings keep the same run
  frozen; Back/Escape return to pause, Resume continues the run.
- Returning to the main menu stops gameplay and clears bombs.
- The M action is unbound. M cannot change sound in menus, settings, pause
  or gameplay.
- Playing HUD shows only three charge icons. Key/mouse hints, room count,
  room names and numbered/tutorial world signs are not rendered.
- The sandbox remains playable, with confetti and no final control prompt.
  The existing R replay binding still starts a fresh run from room one.

Gameplay and physics:
- All four challenges are physically traversable from spawn to exit:
  54 px steps, 260 px ledge, 500 px gap and a 790 px airborne bomb chain.
- Held bomb previews cannot detonate or spend charges. Release creates a
  physical bomb that falls, rebounds and settles on the floor/platform.
- Placement stays within 57 px; terrain and occluded cursor points resolve
  to nearby reachable space. Released bombs cannot be dragged by a new preview.
- Released bombs can detonate while a new preview remains held.
- Charge limits/refills, radial blasts, room transitions and fresh-run replay
  pass. Room replacement clears old collision bodies and all bomb state.
- All five rooms fit one fixed screen. Pause freezes physics and confetti;
  resumed gameplay continues the same state.

Retained visual mechanics:
- Whole-bomb size pulses by +/-5.5% over 1.2 seconds. Five small pixel sparks
  fly from the fuse. Original colors and brightness remain unchanged.
- Bomb bottoms and visible player soles are anchored to the surface,
  including size pulses and landing squash; collision geometry is unchanged.
- Thin rings are absent from explosions, landing, spawn and placement previews.
- Original black eyes/eyebrows follow the held bomb without extra white pupils.
- Short pixel arms grip both bomb sides immediately, return after release,
  and reset correctly on respawn, restart, room load and replay.
