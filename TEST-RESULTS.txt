Gameplay and physics:
- All four challenge rooms are physically traversable from spawn to exit:
  54 px steps, 260 px ledge, 500 px gap and a 790 px airborne bomb chain.
- Held preview has no bomb physics and cannot detonate. Release creates a
  physical bomb that falls, rebounds from the floor and settles on a platform.
- Dragging another preview cannot teleport a released bomb. Existing bombs
  can detonate while a new preview remains held.
- Placement stays within 60 px. Terrain and occluded cursor positions resolve
  to nearby reachable free space; release creates a bomb at the corrected point.
  Only a neighborhood with no accessible point cancels without spending a charge.
  Charge limits, refills, radial blasts and pause pass.
- All five rooms fit one fixed screen. Transitions fade, replace colliders
  without accumulating old bodies, clear bomb states and resume play.
- Fifth room remains freely playable with confetti and unlimited reserve;
  three-active-bomb cap remains. Pause freezes confetti and bomb physics.
- R repeats a challenge; R in the final room starts a fresh run in room one,
  clearing bombs, preview, run statistics and transition state.

Arms and eyes:
- Short pixel arms grip opposite sides of the bomb in the same cursor update,
  without a delayed frame. Hands remain stable while elbow motion stays small.
- Eyes immediately follow the held bomb's direction. Release returns both hands
  and gaze to neutral while the released bomb falls independently.
- Hands and elbows stay finite within a short reach in all target directions.
- Pause freezes joints, gaze and animation clock; gaze relaxes after resuming.
  Teleport, respawn, R, room loading and final replay reset arms and eyes.
- Existing jump and bomb traversal routes still pass after arm integration.
